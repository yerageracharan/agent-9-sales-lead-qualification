Make every service request easier to move forward.

Five independent workflow concepts built around each contractor's publicly visible service process. Each preview uses illustrative records and local-only interactions. Nothing is connected to the company's systems.

Important: These are unsolicited concept demos, not commissioned work, not official sites, and not connected to any real company's website, CRM, email, customers or booking tools. Do not enter real customer information.

The product idea

Request → owner → next action → outcome

A lightweight operations layer that makes the handoff visible without replacing dispatch, pricing, scheduling or the people who serve customers.

Every dashboard value and record below is synthetic demo data.
