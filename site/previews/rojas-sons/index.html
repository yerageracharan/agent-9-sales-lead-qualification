When a guest has no AC, every detail matters.

Imagine a property manager calling about a rental unit before a guest arrives. This walkthrough shows how a service request could carry the property, the people affected, access details and the next update together.

The idea

A property request with its context attached.

A service desk designed around the realities of rental homes and managed properties: units, guest impact, access coordination and keeping the manager informed.

Follow one urgent property request

Five moments. One connected experience.

01 / 05
MOMENT 01 · The moment

A guest is arriving. The rental unit has no cooling.

A property manager needs help with a specific unit, not just a generic AC repair. The team needs to understand who is affected, how urgent it is and how the technician can access the property.

THE MOMENTThe request starts with the property context, not a vague note that says “AC problem.”
MOMENT 02 · The reveal

The request carries the details the team needs.

The property, unit, manager contact, guest arrival time, cooling impact and access instructions appear together. The office can see why the timing matters before deciding the response.

THE SHIFTLess back-and-forth to reconstruct the situation. The important details travel with the request.
MOMENT 03 · The coordination

The office can coordinate the right response.

The request is prioritised for human review. The office checks the service window, confirms access with the property manager and assigns the next action to the appropriate team member.

THE HUMAN MOMENTThe system makes urgency and access visible; the company decides the actual dispatch priority.
MOMENT 04 · The update

The property manager knows what is happening next.

Once the team confirms the plan, the manager can receive a clear update with the expected next step. After service, the record can carry the completion note and any further work required.

THE PAYOFFThe manager is not left wondering whether the request was received, assigned or completed.
MOMENT 05 · The bigger picture

Every managed property has a traceable service history.

The office can see open requests by property, unit and impact. Repeat issues and unresolved access questions are easier to spot, without confusing one rental unit with another.

THE DREAM OUTCOMEA more organised experience for the property manager and a clearer handoff for the service team.
