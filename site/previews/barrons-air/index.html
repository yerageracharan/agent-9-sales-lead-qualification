A future-state workflow · Barron's Air Conditioning

An estimate should never disappear into the inbox.

Imagine a homeowner requesting an estimate for a new AC system. This walkthrough follows that request from the website form to a prepared estimate, a timely follow-up and a clear customer decision.

View current website ↗
The idea

From estimate request to customer decision.

The information already collected at intake becomes the starting point for a visible estimate process, instead of being stranded in a form notification.

Follow one estimate all the way through

Five moments. One connected experience.

01 / 05
MOMENT 01 · The request

A homeowner asks for a new system estimate.

The website form captures the customer's contact details, location, equipment information and referral source. The office has a real opportunity to respond — but first the details need to be reviewed and carried forward.

THE MOMENTThe request becomes an estimate record, not just another email to remember.
MOMENT 02 · The reveal

All the useful details are together.

The estimate card keeps the customer's contact information, service address, ZIP code, equipment type and source beside the request status. Missing details are visible before the team starts preparing the estimate.

THE SHIFTThe office can see what it has and what it still needs, without searching through scattered messages.
MOMENT 03 · The preparation

The team knows which estimate is ready to move.

A staff member checks the information, clarifies any gaps and coordinates the next step needed to prepare the estimate. The system tracks who owns that step.

THE HUMAN MOMENTThe software organises the work. The team determines scope, pricing and what should be offered.
MOMENT 04 · The follow-up

The estimate goes out with a next action attached.

Once the estimate has been sent, the record moves to awaiting customer decision. A follow-up task is scheduled for the date the team chooses. A message can be prepared for review rather than sent without approval.

THE PAYOFFThe estimate stays visible until the customer responds or the team records the outcome.
MOMENT 05 · The decision

Accepted, declined or still considering — the office knows.

When the customer responds, the estimate is marked accordingly. If accepted, the team can move into the company's normal scheduling and installation process. If still pending, the next follow-up remains visible.

THE DREAM OUTCOMEEvery estimate has a clear status, an owner and a next step — without promising every customer will buy.
