A future-state workflow · Ordine's HVAC

A booking request should become a confirmed plan.

Imagine a customer requests an appointment outside normal office hours. The request is not treated as a confirmed appointment until the team checks the details and availability. This walkthrough focuses on the human verification handoff.

View current website ↗
The idea

A clear bridge between booking request and team confirmation.

A staff-facing view for the verification step: what was requested, what needs checking and whether the customer has received a confirmed plan.

From requested time to confirmed appointment

Five moments. One connected experience.

01 / 05
MOMENT 01 · The moment

A customer requests an appointment after the office closes.

The customer shares the service needed and a preferred time. The request is captured, but it is not yet a confirmed appointment. The team still needs to verify the details and capacity.

THE MOMENTThe customer knows the request was received. The business keeps control of the actual appointment confirmation.
MOMENT 02 · The reveal

The next morning, the team sees exactly what needs review.

The request appears in a verification queue with customer contact, service type, preferred window and any missing information. Nothing is silently treated as confirmed.

THE SHIFTThe office starts with a prioritised list of decisions instead of reconstructing requests from messages.
MOMENT 03 · The decision

A team member checks availability before confirming.

The employee checks the real schedule and service capacity, clarifies details if needed and chooses an available appointment window. The software supports the handoff; it does not invent availability.

THE HUMAN MOMENTOnly a human-approved slot becomes a confirmed appointment.
MOMENT 04 · The confirmation

The customer receives a clear answer.

Once the team approves the appointment, the customer receives the confirmed details. If the requested time is unavailable, the team can offer an alternative and track the response.

THE PAYOFFRequested, pending and confirmed are three different states — and everyone can see which one applies.
MOMENT 05 · The safeguard

Urgent situations follow the right path.

A request that indicates an emergency is flagged for the company's immediate phone-based process rather than being left in an ordinary appointment-verification queue.

THE DREAM OUTCOMEA tidy booking handoff without confusing a request for an appointment with a confirmed dispatch.
