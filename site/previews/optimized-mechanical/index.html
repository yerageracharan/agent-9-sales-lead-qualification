A future-state workflow · Optimized Mechanical

What if every request had a next step?

Picture one busy morning: residential repairs, commercial service and construction enquiries are all moving at once. This walkthrough shows one possible way the office could keep each request moving without losing sight of the next decision.

View current website ↗
The idea

One view of the work. One clear next move.

A request-to-estimate flow that gives the office a shared picture across different work types, while people stay in control of scope, estimates and scheduling.

From incoming request to a visible next move

Five moments. One connected experience.

01 / 05
MOMENT 01 · The busy morning

Three kinds of work arrive. The office still has one place to look.

A homeowner asks about a system replacement. A commercial customer needs service. A builder asks about a project. Each request has a different path, but the office needs to know what happens next for all three.

THE MOMENTInstead of relying on separate notes and memory, every request enters the same visual flow.
MOMENT 02 · The reveal

Each request arrives with the context that matters.

The request is grouped by work type and service area. The team can see what the customer needs, what information is missing and who is responsible for the next action.

THE SHIFTResidential, commercial and construction work become easier to distinguish without creating three disconnected processes.
MOMENT 03 · The handoff

The office sees the next decision, not just a name in a list.

One request needs a scope clarification. Another is ready for a site visit. A third is waiting for an estimate to be prepared. Each has an owner and a specific next step.

THE HUMAN MOMENTThe system surfaces the handoff. The employee decides what to do and when.
MOMENT 04 · The follow-through

An estimate sent is not the end of the story.

After an estimate is sent, the request moves into a decision stage with a follow-up date. If the date arrives without an outcome, the office can see that the next conversation is due.

THE PAYOFFOpen work stays visible until it is booked, declined or deliberately closed.
MOMENT 05 · The new normal

The owner can see where work is waiting.

The morning view shows new requests, site visits to coordinate and estimates awaiting customer decisions. It doesn't replace judgement or dispatch. It makes the next move easier to see.

THE DREAM OUTCOMEA calmer handoff across job types, with fewer requests depending on someone remembering a sticky note.
