A future-state workflow · 3H AC of Tampa

Two maintenance visits. Nothing left to memory.

Imagine an agreement starting today. Six months later, the first visit is due. Six months after that, the second. This walkthrough shows how a team could see each agreement through both visits and into the next renewal conversation.

The idea

Every agreement has a timeline, not just a promise.

A two-visit fulfilment view that connects the agreement, due windows, scheduling, service checklists and completion status.

Follow an agreement through its full year

Five moments. One connected experience.

01 / 05
MOMENT 01 · The agreement

A customer signs up for preventive maintenance.

The agreement is recorded against the customer's property and the covered HVAC system. The two maintenance visits become visible milestones rather than details buried in a document.

THE MOMENTThe office can see what was promised and which system each visit belongs to.
MOMENT 02 · The reveal

Both visits appear on the calendar from day one.

The first visit is due around six months into the agreement. The second follows around six months later. The dates can be adjusted to the agreement terms and the customer's availability.

THE SHIFTThe second visit is not forgotten just because the first one was completed.
MOMENT 03 · The first visit

When the first visit is due, the right checklist is ready.

The office sees the agreement approaching its first visit window and can start scheduling. The technician receives the appropriate service checklist for the first maintenance visit.

THE HUMAN MOMENTThe visit is scheduled, assigned and completed against the actual agreement requirements.
MOMENT 04 · The second visit

The next visit is already waiting in the timeline.

After visit one is completed, the agreement moves forward to its second service window. If it becomes overdue or needs rescheduling, that status is visible to the office.

THE PAYOFFOne completed visit does not make the whole annual agreement look complete.
MOMENT 05 · The renewal

The team can see which agreements need attention next.

As the agreement approaches its end date, the office can prepare a renewal conversation and check whether both visits have been fulfilled. The customer remains in control of whether to renew.

THE DREAM OUTCOMEA clearer fulfilment process: two visits tracked, overdue work visible and renewal timing easier to manage.
