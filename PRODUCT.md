# Product brief

## What this is
Fieldwork is a collection of independent, tailored future-state workflow demonstrations for five US HVAC contractors. It is not a SaaS product, a commissioned website or a live implementation.

## Audience
Business owners, general managers and office managers at independent HVAC contractors. They understand service requests, estimates, dispatch, appointment booking, property access and maintenance agreements. They do not need a generic software tour.

## The visitor's job
Recognise a situation from a normal working day, follow one realistic scenario and imagine how the office/customer experience could improve if the handoffs were clear.

## Primary action
Explore a guided workflow and decide whether the scenario is relevant enough to discuss. Do not collect fake leads or make the visitor fill out a pretend form.

## Product story
Each walkthrough should show:
1. A familiar operational moment.
2. The information and decision that matter.
3. How the work is handed to the right person.
4. What the customer or office sees next.
5. The intended operating outcome, without claiming measured ROI.

## Trust boundaries
- All names, records, dates, times and statuses inside the scenario are fictional.
- Do not imply that the named contractor uses this software, has this problem, or has approved the concept.
- Do not claim integrations, automation, guaranteed results or revenue lift without evidence.
- Respect each company's existing systems. The concept is a starting hypothesis for discovery, not a diagnosis.
- Keep human approval for scope, price, availability, dispatch and customer communication unless a future implementation establishes otherwise.

## Success criteria
A prospect can explain the problem and the intended workflow after a short walkthrough. The experience feels specific to the business and industry, not like a generic SaaS dashboard.
